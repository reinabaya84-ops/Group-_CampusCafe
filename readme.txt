PROJECT TITLE: Common Grounds Café

PROJECT THEME:

Our website features a welcoming campus café where students 
and coffee enthusiasts can take a break, enjoy freshly brewed coffee, 
and choose from a variety of delicious food and treats. 
It highlights a comfortable space where people can relax, study, and spend time with others.
