WHO WE ARE

Every cup has a story.

Common Grounds was created with one simple idea: to bring people together through good coffee and comfortable spaces.

We want our café to be a place where friends can catch up, students can take a break, and everyone can enjoy a moment for themselves.

More than serving drinks and food, we hope to create experiences worth remembering.
